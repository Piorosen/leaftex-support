# LeafTeX Support

LeafTeX is a LaTeX editor for iPhone and iPad. It typesets your documents on
the device and works with projects on the device, in iCloud Drive, in Git
repositories and on FTP, SFTP and SSH servers.

## Contact

Questions, problems and suggestions:
**[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Piorosen/leaftex-support/issues/new)**.
Please include your device, its iOS version, the LeafTeX version and the steps
that lead to the problem. Don't post passwords, API keys or private documents.

## Common questions

**Where are my projects?**
Where you created them: on the device (Files › On My iPhone/iPad › LeafTeX),
in iCloud Drive › LeafTeX, or in a folder you linked from the Files app.

**Does typesetting need the internet?**
No. LeafTeX turns your LaTeX into PDF on the device, as you type. Errors and
warnings are listed under Problems; tap one to go to its line.

**How do I work with a Git repository?**
New Project › Git Repository, with an HTTPS address and, for a private
repository, a personal access token. Sync commits your changes, pulls and
pushes.

**How do I work with files on a server?**
New Project › FTP, SFTP or SSH Server. LeafTeX copies the folder to the device;
Sync sends changes both ways.

**What does the AI assistant send, and where?**
Nothing until you choose a model and allow it. On-device models (Apple
Intelligence, or a model you download) keep your text on the device. With your
own server (Ollama or an OpenAI-compatible server) or a cloud provider (Claude
or OpenAI, with your own API key), LeafTeX asks before the first request. You
can revoke this in Settings › AI Assistant.

## LeafTeX Pro

**What does LeafTeX Pro add?**
The free version keeps up to 2 projects of up to 30 files each, on the
device or in folders from the Files app. LeafTeX Pro adds unlimited
projects and files, iCloud Drive, Git, FTP, SFTP and SSH, the AI assistant,
Beamer slides with presentation mode, and engine notes in Problems.

**How do I buy it, or get it back on a new device?**
Settings › LeafTeX Pro, or tap any Pro feature. It is a one-time
purchase, not a subscription. It works on all your devices with the same
Apple Account and with your family through Family Sharing. Tap **Restore
Purchases** on a new device. For refunds, see
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Privacy

Read the [privacy policy](privacy/). LeafTeX has no accounts, analytics,
advertising or tracking.
